# What this site collects

Plainly: what we measure, what you send us, and who else handles it.

*Last updated October 28, 2026*

## How we measure the site

We use [Microsoft Clarity](https://clarity.microsoft.com/) to see how people use this site: which pages they open, where they click and how far they scroll. Clarity also records sessions as replays, along with your browser, device, approximate location and the page that sent you here. It may set cookies to tell one visit from the next.

We look at this to find what's confusing or broken and fix it. We don't sell it. Microsoft handles the data under the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

## When you contact us

The [contact form](https://sawirstudio.com/contact/) sends your name, email, company (if you add one), what you need and your message to a Google Form in our Google account. We use it to reply and to talk about your project. Ask us and we'll delete it.

## Services the site loads

The fonts come from Google Fonts, so your browser shares your IP address with Google when it fetches them ([Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)). The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which handles every request on its way to you ([Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)).

## Stored in your browser

If you switch between light and dark mode, your browser remembers the choice in its local storage. It stays on your device and isn't sent to us.

## Our apps

Each of our apps has its own privacy policy, linked from its App Store listing. See them all under [Projects](https://sawirstudio.com/projects/).

## Questions

For a question about your data, or to have it deleted, write to us through the [contact form](https://sawirstudio.com/contact/).
