Sawir Studio Is Now the Publisher of Colvaron Conquest

Sawir Studio is now the publisher of Colvaron Conquest, the strategic trading card game created by Coco JM. We run and sell the game at colvaronconquest.com, and you can play it free in your browser today.

What Colvaron Conquest is

Colvaron is a world where magic and advanced technology collide. It began in Coco's childhood imagination and became a card game in 2025, played with five elements and a two-dice action system. There are more than 200 cards, each with its own page in the card gallery, and fifteen stories about the continent, its kingdoms and factions.

How you can play

  • Against the AI, to learn the elements and try decks.
  • Two players on one device, passing it across the table.
  • Online, against friends or in quick and rated matches.

It runs in any modern browser. On a phone you can add it to your home screen and play from there, even offline. New players can start with the tutorials.

What's free

The game is free to play. Coins and the Octastar's Blessing, a 30-day pass that never renews by itself, are optional purchases, paid by card through Stripe. Questions about payments and refunds are answered on the game's support page.

What it means for us

We designed and built the game's website first, then the online game. As its publisher we now also run it: payments, player support and updates. Colvaron and its cards are Coco JM's. Our job is to get them to players.

It sits next to the apps we make ourselves, like Squishu and Pawlitics, and we handle it the same way. If you'd like to sponsor an event or a tournament, the game's about page tells you how.
