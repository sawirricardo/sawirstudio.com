# Built to be found.

We build it so it can be found, then we help people find it. Search, listings and launch, done by the people who built the site, so the fixes actually ship.

[Start a project](https://sawirstudio.com/contact/)

## What's included

- ### Search set up from day one

  Titles and descriptions, sitemaps, structured data and fast pages, built in while we build. For a site we didn't build, we start with an audit and fix what it finds.
- ### Local search

  Pages aimed at what people nearby actually type, like “dokter kulit Mataram”, a tidy Google Business Profile, and booking that goes straight to WhatsApp.
- ### Launch listings & backlinks

  Your product listed where early users and links come from: launch sites, directories, deal lists, comparison pages, marketplaces and roundups we pitch to. Every link is earned, never bought.
- ### Store listings

  App Store and WordPress.org pages with names, descriptions, icons and banners that people actually click.
- ### A monthly check-in

  A short report from your own Search Console data: what moved, what didn't, and the next few changes we'll make.

## How it works

1. ### Set up at launch.

   Search basics go in while we build, not after. If we didn't build your site, we audit it first.
2. ### Get it listed.

   We submit and tidy your listings, so people and search engines can find you from the first week.
3. ### Check in every month.

   We read your Search Console numbers with you, then pick and ship the next few changes.

What we won't promise

We don't guarantee rankings or traffic numbers. Nobody honestly can. We promise the work, done properly, and real numbers from your own Search Console every month, good or bad.
