## Is custom worth it?

Custom costs more up front than a subscription, so it has to earn its place. It usually does when:

- **The way you work is your edge.** A pricing model, a service or a process your competitors don't have, which a generic tool would flatten.
- **You're paying for four tools and gluing them with spreadsheets.** One system that does exactly your job often costs less within a couple of years.
- **Your customers need an app from you**, not a link to someone else's platform.

It doesn't when a standard tool already does the job, and we'll say so. If what you need is point of sale, stock and accounting, start with [Odoo](https://sawirstudio.com/odoo/) and build only what it's missing.

## How it works

1. ### Start small, ship early.

   We pick the one part that matters most and put a working version in front of your real people early, then sharpen it with what they actually do.
2. ### See it as it grows.

   You use it while it's being built, not at the end. AI does much of the typing, which is why we're fast; a person reads, tests and answers for every line.
3. ### Launch is the start.

   Once it's live, we stay on to fix, update and add to it, for as long as you need.
