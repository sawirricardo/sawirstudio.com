# OpenAI Will Pay for Your AI Coach: What OpenWorks Covers, and What It Doesn't

On November 23, OpenAI [launched a pilot called OpenWorks](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2124922133545714160). It pays local AI experts, called OpenWorks Partners, a monthly stipend of up to $8,000 to give independently owned small businesses in the US free, hands-on help with AI. The businesses also get three months of ChatGPT Business at no cost.

It's a good program, and if you qualify, you should apply. It also says something about where AI in small businesses really stands.

## What's in it

- **Who gets help:** independently owned US small businesses, such as restaurants, retailers, service firms and manufacturers. Software and SaaS companies, venture-backed businesses and private equity-owned businesses can't apply. No technical experience is needed.
- **What they get:** one-on-one support from a partner on a project the owner chooses, practice with the tools they already use, and the three free months of ChatGPT Business. OpenAI's examples of projects are reaching new customers, launching ad campaigns and building tools a team can keep using.
- **What partners do:** spend days each month with local businesses and host a monthly workshop. [ForkLog reports](https://forklog.com/en/openai-launches-openworks-offers-up-to-8000-a-month-to-coach-small-businesses-on-ai/) that the application asks for four full workdays a month, a US base and in-person availability. Individuals, small teams and software or AI consultancies can apply alongside their existing clients.
- **Where:** 20 communities, including New York, Chicago, Atlanta, Houston, Austin, Nashville, Seattle, Los Angeles and Fargo.
- **How long:** OpenAI hasn't said how long the pilot runs.

Businesses and partners both apply on [OpenAI's OpenWorks page](https://openai.com/openworks). Apply there and nowhere else: a free program with a famous name is an easy target for fake offers.

## Why OpenAI is paying for it

Because a license on its own doesn't change how a business runs. Plenty of owners have tried ChatGPT. Far fewer have changed how the orders, the stock or the Monday report get done, because nobody had the time to sit down and work it into the day. OpenWorks pays someone to sit down with them.

It's also good business for OpenAI. Three free months of ChatGPT Business, with an expert showing the team how to use it, is a strong reason to keep paying in month four. Nothing wrong with that, but expect the help to center on ChatGPT.

## What coaching gets you

Coaching is the right help when the goal is for your own people to use AI well: writing and answering faster, running ads, sorting the inbox, a custom assistant or a small tool the team keeps using. The owner and the staff learn, and the skills stay in the business.

It reaches its limit when the problem is in the systems themselves:

- **Systems that don't talk.** A store, an accounting system and a few spreadsheets that someone retypes between. Making [Shopify payouts match your books](https://sawirstudio.com/posts/why-shopify-payouts-dont-match-your-books/) is engineering work, not a workshop.
- **AI that acts in your real data.** An assistant that reads and changes your orders or your stock has to respect who's allowed to see and change what. Someone has to build that and answer for it.
- **Next year.** A coach's days end. Software has to be watched, fixed and updated after the program is over.
