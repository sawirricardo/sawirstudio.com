One system, from the till to the books.

Point of sale, stock, purchasing and accounting in one place. On Odoo when it fits how you work, built to measure when it doesn't, and we tell you which before you spend anything.

What we do

  • Odoo, set up for you

    Point of sale, inventory, purchasing, sales and accounting, configured to the way your business already runs, not the other way round. We know Odoo's insides because we write modules for it.

  • Every shop on one count

    Each outlet and warehouse on the same stock, with sizes and colours as variants, barcodes, transfers between stores and a reorder point that tells you before you run out.

  • Online sales in the same stock

    Your Shopify store through the open-source connector we wrote, and your website or other channels through their APIs, so you never sell what you no longer have.

  • Built to measure

    When the way you work is what sets you apart, we build the system around it: a web app for the office, an app for your staff or customers, and the admin behind them. You own the code and the data. See custom software.

  • Odoo, plus your own pieces

    Often Odoo covers almost everything and one part of your work needs something of its own. We keep Odoo as the core and build the one module, app or report it doesn't have.

  • Moving in

    Products, customers, suppliers, opening stock and balances brought over from spreadsheets or your old software, and the people at the counter trained before go-live.

Odoo or custom?

It's the first question everyone asks, and the honest answer depends on how unusual your business really is.

  • Odoo when your flow is what most shops, distributors and restaurants do: sell, restock, pay suppliers, close the month. It's faster and cheaper, it's open source, and Odoo Community has no licence fee. You bend a little to fit it.
  • Built to measure when the system is the business: a pricing model, an approval chain or a service Odoo would fight at every step. It costs more up front and fits exactly.
  • Both when Odoo covers the everyday and one part of your work is yours alone. This is the most common answer.

We map your flow first and then recommend one, in writing, with what each would cost. Whichever you choose, it's built on open, mainstream tools that any good developer can pick up, so you're never locked in to us.

How it works

  1. Walk the floor first.

    We follow a sale, a delivery, a stock count and a month-end the way they happen today, and write down where things get lost.

  2. One part at a time.

    Usually the till and stock first, then purchasing, then accounting. Each runs on your real data before the next begins, so the shop never stops.

  3. We stay.

    New outlets, new reports, Odoo version upgrades and the questions that come up on a busy Saturday. PeopleBond has worked with us since 2019.

What we won't do

Sell you a system you don't need. If Odoo's standard apps cover it, we'll say so and keep the custom work out. If your flow really is different, we won't force it into Odoo just because that's easier for us.

Ricardo Sawir, founder
