# Glad you found us

A studio that ships fast, with AI in the loop and a human owning every call.

A note from the founder

I started SawirStudio to build apps, websites and designs that hold up for the people who use them. Today we build with AI every day. It writes a lot of the first drafts, and that's how SawirStudio put six of its own apps on the App Store in under four weeks.

A tool can't own a decision, though, so we do. We read the code, test it on real devices and answer for what ships. Client work gets the same deal.

[Ricardo Sawir](https://sawirstudio.com/ricardosawir/), founder

## What we believe

1. ### Ship, then sharpen.

   We'd rather put something real in front of people early than polish it in private. Six of our own apps went from idea to the App Store in under four weeks.
2. ### AI does the typing, we do the thinking.

   We build with AI every day. It's why we're fast and why we cost less than you'd expect. A person still reads, tests and answers for every line that ships.
3. ### Plain tech.

   We pick the simplest tool that does the job. This site is plain HTML and CSS. Fewer moving parts means less to break, and less to pay for later.
4. ### We use what we make.

   [Par](https://sawirstudio.com/projects/par/), [Rindraw](https://sawirstudio.com/projects/rindraw/), [Squishu](https://sawirstudio.com/projects/squishu/) and the rest are our own products. We handle their launches, App Review and support ourselves, so we know that work from the inside, not just from client projects.
5. ### Say what's happening.

   Clear privacy policies, honest notes on what data an app sends, and no dark patterns. In our apps and in client work.

## Working with us

We like to work in partnership, as advisors and as architects of technically hard projects. Tell us what you're dealing with, and give us as much input as you have, so we can get it right early.

Tools we reach for: Laravel, Livewire, Go, Swift, Flutter, React, Vue, Tailwind, PostgreSQL and MySQL. We pick per project, and plain HTML when that's enough.
