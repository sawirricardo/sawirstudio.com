# Vivaldy Skin Clinic Website

![Vivaldy Skin Clinic Website](https://sawirstudio.com/assets/images/projects/vivaldy-skin-clinic-website.webp)

## The client

Vivaldy Skin Clinic is a dermatology and venereology clinic in Mataram, Lombok, led by dr. Dedianto Hidajat, Sp.DVE. Its patients rate it 4.9 from 150 reviews on Google.

## What we made

We designed and built the clinic’s website, presenting its treatments, shop and contact details, and have looked after it since 2022.

- **Treatments.** A page for each of its 21 treatments, from facials to lasers, fillers and botox.
- **Booking.** Every page leads to a consultation booked on WhatsApp.
- **The doctor.** An introduction to dr. Dedianto Hidajat and the clinic’s idea that no two skins are the same.
- **Answers.** Opening hours, frequently asked questions about downtime, booking and prices, and patient reviews.
- **Promos, blog and shop.** Current promotions, articles, and the clinic’s skincare shop.

## How we approached it

Most patients find the clinic by searching for a skin specialist in Mataram, and in Indonesia they book by chat, not by form. So page titles and descriptions target local searches, and the main button on every page opens WhatsApp. We first built the site in Next.js, and later rebuilt it as fast static pages on Cloudflare.

**Built with** HTML, Tailwind CSS and Cloudflare
