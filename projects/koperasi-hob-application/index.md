# Koperasi HOB Application

Everyday grocery shopping app for a cooperative

![Koperasi HOB app banner](https://sawirstudio.com/assets/images/projects/koperasi-hob-application.webp)

## The client

Koperasi HOB is a cooperative that sells everyday household goods to its members through its own stores.

## What we made

We built the whole shop: the members’ app, the API behind it, and the admin panel the cooperative runs it from. The app went live on the App Store in December 2025.

- **Shopping.** Members browse products by category and sub-category, save favorites, and can ask the cooperative to stock something it doesn’t carry yet.
- **Checkout.** Delivery or pick-up. Delivery is priced by shipping zone, matched to the Indonesian region of the address.
- **Payment.** Online through iPaymu or by bank transfer, and the order updates itself when an iPaymu payment comes through.
- **Stock per store.** Each store keeps its own stock, and an admin can close shopping at one store without touching the others.
- **Admin.** Staff manage products, categories, featured products, orders and members, and print any order as a PDF.

## How we approached it

A cooperative’s members are neighbours and colleagues, not anonymous shoppers, so the app keeps the steps few: pick products, choose delivery or pick-up, pay. The harder work is behind it. Stock is tracked per store and checked again at checkout, so nobody pays for something that has just sold out, and shipping costs come from zones the cooperative sets up itself instead of a courier’s price list.

**Built with** Expo (React Native), Laravel, Filament and iPaymu
