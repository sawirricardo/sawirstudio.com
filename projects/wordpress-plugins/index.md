# WordPress Plugins

![Icons of Sawir Studio’s twelve WordPress plugins](https://sawirstudio.com/assets/images/projects/wordpress-plugins.webp)

## The project

WordPress runs a large share of the web, and WooCommerce runs many of its shops. We publish free plugins for both on WordPress.org, each one doing a single job well, so a site owner can add what they need without buying a bundle of everything else. Every plugin below has its own page with its features and answers to common questions.

## For WooCommerce shops

- **[Sawir Renew](https://sawirstudio.com/projects/wordpress-plugins/sawir-renew/).** Subscriptions for WooCommerce: recurring billing, trials, sign-up fees, pause and skip, plan switching, dunning and smart retries, all free.
- **[Sawir Referral](https://sawirstudio.com/projects/wordpress-plugins/sawir-referral/).** A give-and-get referral program: friends get a discount, advocates earn a reward coupon, shared by code or link, WhatsApp first.
- **[Sawir Pulse](https://sawirstudio.com/projects/wordpress-plugins/sawir-pulse/).** Social proof from real activity only: recent orders, live viewers and reviews as small corner notifications.
- **[Sawir Restock Notifier](https://sawirstudio.com/projects/wordpress-plugins/sawir-restock-notifier-for-woocommerce/).** Customers ask to be emailed when an out-of-stock product is back.
- **[Sawir Min Max Quantities](https://sawirstudio.com/projects/wordpress-plugins/sawir-min-max-quantities-for-woocommerce/).** Minimum and maximum quantity rules per product, per category and for the whole order.
- **[Sawir Product Image Zoom](https://sawirstudio.com/projects/wordpress-plugins/sawir-product-image-zoom-for-woocommerce/).** A lightbox with navigation and click-to-zoom for product galleries.
- **[Sawir Variation Gallery Tools](https://sawirstudio.com/projects/wordpress-plugins/sawir-variation-gallery-tools-woocommerce/).** Gallery images per product variation, working together with Product Image Zoom.

## For any WordPress site

- **[Sawir Insights](https://sawirstudio.com/projects/wordpress-plugins/sawir-insights/).** Heatmaps, session recordings, surveys, funnels and form analytics, with everything kept on your own server.
- **[Sawir Social Scheduler](https://sawirstudio.com/projects/wordpress-plugins/sawir-social-scheduler/).** Write once and schedule to 27 networks from wp-admin, with per-channel edits, threads, media and previews.
- **[Sawir Live Translate](https://sawirstudio.com/projects/wordpress-plugins/sawir-live-translate/).** Translate any front-end page live, save the strings, and let visitors switch languages.
- **[Sawir Media Folders](https://sawirstudio.com/projects/wordpress-plugins/sawir-media-folders/).** Fast, unlimited folders for the media library, with drag and drop.
- **[Sawir Role Capabilities Manager](https://sawirstudio.com/projects/wordpress-plugins/sawir-role-capabilities-manager/).** Create custom user roles and manage what every role can do.

## How we approached it

Plugins live inside other people’s businesses, so we hold them to a few rules. Each plugin is free on WordPress.org and does one job. Data stays on the site owner’s server: Insights records sessions without a third-party service, and Social Scheduler keeps its tokens in WordPress. And nothing pretends: Pulse only shows activity that really happened, and Renew gives away the subscription features others charge for.

**Built with** PHP, WordPress and WooCommerce
