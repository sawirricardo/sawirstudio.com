WordPress Plugins

The project

WordPress runs a large share of the web, and WooCommerce runs many of its shops. We publish free plugins for both on WordPress.org, each one doing a single job well, so a site owner can add what they need without buying a bundle of everything else. Every plugin below has its own page with its features and answers to common questions.

For WooCommerce shops

  • Sawir Renew. Subscriptions for WooCommerce: recurring billing, trials, sign-up fees, pause and skip, plan switching, dunning and smart retries, all free.
  • Sawir Referral. A give-and-get referral program: friends get a discount, advocates earn a reward coupon, shared by code or link, WhatsApp first.
  • Sawir Pulse. Social proof from real activity only: recent orders, live viewers and reviews as small corner notifications.
  • Sawir Restock Notifier. Customers ask to be emailed when an out-of-stock product is back.
  • Sawir Min Max Quantities. Minimum and maximum quantity rules per product, per category and for the whole order.
  • Sawir Product Image Zoom. A lightbox with navigation and click-to-zoom for product galleries.
  • Sawir Variation Gallery Tools. Gallery images per product variation, working together with Product Image Zoom.

For any WordPress site

  • Sawir Insights. Heatmaps, session recordings, surveys, funnels and form analytics, with everything kept on your own server.
  • Sawir Social Scheduler. Write once and schedule to 27 networks from wp-admin, with per-channel edits, threads, media and previews.
  • Sawir Live Translate. Translate any front-end page live, save the strings, and let visitors switch languages.
  • Sawir Media Folders. Fast, unlimited folders for the media library, with drag and drop.
  • Sawir Role Capabilities Manager. Create custom user roles and manage what every role can do.

How we approached it

Plugins live inside other people’s businesses, so we hold them to a few rules. Each plugin is free on WordPress.org and does one job. Data stays on the site owner’s server: Insights records sessions without a third-party service, and Social Scheduler keeps its tokens in WordPress. And nothing pretends: Pulse only shows activity that really happened, and Renew gives away the subscription features others charge for.
