# Sawir Restock Notifier

![Sawir Restock Notifier banner](https://sawirstudio.com/assets/images/plugins/sawir-restock-notifier-for-woocommerce-banner.webp)

Sawir Restock Notifier lets customers leave their email on an out-of-stock product and tells them, once, when it is back.

[Get it free on WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/sawir-restock-notifier-for-woocommerce/)

## What it does

Sawir Restock Notifier for WooCommerce adds a lightweight stock alert form to out-of-stock product pages. Customers enter an email address, confirm the request by email, and then receive a one-time email when the product or selected variation returns to stock.

## Features

- Displays a restock notification form on out-of-stock product stock notices.
- Supports simple products and product variations.
- Supports guest customers and logged-in customers.
- Includes an optional setting to require login before customers can sign up for restock alerts.
- Uses double opt-in confirmation before activating a restock alert.
- Includes cancel and unsubscribe links in customer emails.
- Lets admins resend verification emails for unconfirmed requests.
- Adds a My Account > Restock Alerts page where logged-in customers can view, cancel, unsubscribe, and resend verification for alerts created while signed in.
- Stores awaiting confirmation, confirmed, notified, and unsubscribed requests in a custom database table.
- Sends WooCommerce-styled restock emails when stock status changes back to in stock.
- Processes restock notification emails in background batches with WooCommerce Action Scheduler.
- Adds a WooCommerce > Restock Alerts admin page for reviewing requests.
- Shows an admin insights dashboard with range filters, status totals, rates, and top requested products.
- Includes an optional uninstall cleanup setting for removing plugin data when the plugin is deleted.
- Asks admins during deactivation whether plugin data should be kept or destructively removed if the plugin is deleted later.

## Questions

### How does it compare to alternatives?

Waitlist suites like YITH WooCommerce Waiting List and the commercial back-in-stock extensions add dashboards, exportable waitlists, SMS, and marketing integrations. Sawir Restock Notifier keeps to the essential loop: a small form on out-of-stock products, a double opt-in confirmation, and one email when the product or variation returns. If you plan to market to your waitlist, choose a suite; if you simply don't want customers walking away from an out-of-stock page, this does exactly that.

### Does this require WooCommerce?

Yes. This plugin is built for WooCommerce products and stock status hooks.

### Does it notify customers more than once?

No. Each request is marked as notified after a restock email is sent.

### Does it support double opt-in?

Yes. Customers must click the confirmation link before the restock alert becomes active.
