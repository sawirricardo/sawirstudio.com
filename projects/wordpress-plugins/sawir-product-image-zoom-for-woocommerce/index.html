Sawir Product Image Zoom

Sawir Product Image Zoom opens your product photos in a lightbox, with arrows to move through the gallery and a click to zoom in on the details.

Get it free on WordPress.org

What it does

Sawir Product Image Zoom for WooCommerce replaces the default WooCommerce product gallery image click behavior with a focused lightbox experience.

Features:

  • Opens product images in a full-screen lightbox.
  • Supports previous and next navigation.
  • Supports keyboard navigation with Escape, left arrow, and right arrow keys.
  • Supports swipe navigation on touch devices.
  • Adds click-to-zoom and drag-to-pan while zoomed.
  • Works with classic WooCommerce product galleries and block-based product galleries.

The plugin only loads its CSS and JavaScript on single product pages.

Questions

How does it compare to alternatives?

Gallery suites like Iconic WooThumbs and YITH Zoom Magnifier replace the whole WooCommerce product gallery: layouts, sliders, video, and zoom together. Sawir Product Image Zoom changes one thing — what happens when a customer clicks an image. Your theme's gallery stays as it is, and customers get a fast lightbox with keyboard, swipe, and click-to-zoom, with assets loaded only on product pages. If you want to redesign the gallery itself, use a suite; if the gallery is fine and only the viewing experience is lacking, this is the lighter path.

Does this require WooCommerce?

Yes. This plugin is built for WooCommerce product galleries.

Does it load assets site-wide?

No. Assets load only on WooCommerce single product pages.

Does it support touch devices?

Yes. The lightbox supports swipe navigation and zoomed image panning on touch devices.
