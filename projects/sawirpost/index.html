SawirPost

Write a post once and publish it to 25+ social channels on schedule, built for agencies

Write a post once and SawirPost publishes it to 25+ channels on your schedule: spaced out naturally, tracked per channel, and approved by your client before it ships.

Channels cover big networks, niche communities, blogs and newsletters, including X, Instagram, Facebook Pages, Threads, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Bluesky, Mastodon, Reddit, Discord, Telegram, WordPress.com and Dev.to.

It is built for agency work. Clients review each post through a private preview link and comment right on it, channels are grouped by client so nothing ships to the wrong brand, and posts can be staggered minutes apart or repeated on a cadence.
