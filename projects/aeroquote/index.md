# Aeroquote

Air charter quotes for operators and brokers, still on sale four years on

![The aeroquote.com home page and an Aeroquote quote document cover](https://sawirstudio.com/assets/images/projects/aeroquote.webp)

## The result

- **Still on sale.** Aeroquote sells to air charter operators and brokers today, at [aeroquote.com](https://aeroquote.com/).
- **Brokers as well as operators.** We built the broker plan and its limits, and brokers are one of the two groups Aeroquote sells to.
- **The website we built in 2022 is still its front door,** pricing page and free trial signup included.

## The situation

Aeroquote is air charter software: operators and brokers use it to price charter flights and send quotes. A charter quote has to be accurate, down to the positioning flights to and from the aircraft’s home base, and it has to look good enough to win the booking.

## The task

Help Aeroquote build out the product in 2022: quoting, operators and their fleets, the documents customers receive, and a plan for brokers. And give it a website that turns visitors into free trials.

## What we did

From March to September 2022 we worked on the Aeroquote app with Aeroquote’s team, and built its marketing site.

- **Quotes.** Generating quotes, the page a customer sees when they open one, and quote request emails that carry the operator’s details.
- **Quote documents in the operator’s own style.** A PDF made from the operator’s own Word template, and the design of the quote document itself.
- **Operators and fleets.** Operator groups, aircraft, aircraft from other operators, and airports on a map.
- **A plan for brokers,** with its own limits, next to the operator plans.
- **Getting started.** Onboarding steps, the dashboard, a calendar, date filters and in-app support.
- **The website.** Features, pricing for operators and brokers, a free trial signup and the way into the app.
