NoFapFit

An instant exercise challenge for the moment an urge hits, with streaks to build discipline

NoFapFit turns an urge into a workout. When temptation hits, you press one big red button and get a random exercise with a set rep count: push-ups, squats, burpees or planks. The energy goes into your body instead of into guilt.

Every challenge you finish adds to your streak, and a weekly view shows your sessions and top exercises, with a leaderboard for the longest streaks. Iron Will Pro unlocks all 16 exercises, weighted workouts like bench press, deadlifts and kettlebell swings, and deeper stats.
