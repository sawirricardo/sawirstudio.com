PeopleBond Connect App

HR in every employee’s pocket, with attendance that’s hard to fake

The result

  • HR moved to the phone. Employees tap in, ask for leave and download payslips in pbConnect, on the App Store and Google Play, instead of filling in a form and chasing a signature.
  • Attendance that’s hard to fake. Every tap in checks where the employee is, can ask for a face, and won’t run on a jailbroken or rooted phone.
  • Five years of steady releases. From the first build in November 2021 to break-time reminders in 2026, the app keeps growing with the product.

The situation

Attendance is where HR software gets tested every single day. On paper it means forms, signatures and HR mornings spent chasing both. And attendance is where employees are most tempted to cheat: a friend taps in for you, or you tap in from home.

The task

Put everyday HR in each employee’s pocket, and make attendance something a company can trust without getting in an honest employee’s way.

What we did

  • Tap in and out. Attendance with geotagging, a selfie checked by on-device face detection where the company turns it on, or a QR code at the office that expires.
  • Requests and approvals. Leave, absence, overtime, shift changes, business trips and attendance claims, with approvals for managers and a view of their team.
  • Pay and company info. Payslips, the working calendar, the organisation structure, company policies and news.
  • Notifications. Push notifications and an inbox, so a request never waits unseen.
  • Two languages. English and Indonesian.

How it grew

  • 2021. Work starts in November: sign-in, tap in by QR code, the working calendar and a manager’s view of their team.
  • 2022. Leave, absence and overtime requests with approvals, push notifications, a photo at tap in, and timesheets. The app goes live on the App Store and Google Play.
  • 2023. Payslip downloads, a tap history showing where each tap happened, English and Indonesian, business trip approvals, and QR codes that expire.
  • 2024. Shift change requests and on-device face detection for the tap in selfie, then a full upgrade of the app underneath, so it keeps up with new phones.
  • 2026. Break times with reminders, a rebuilt team view with filters, a new home screen, and scripted releases that build and sign both apps.
