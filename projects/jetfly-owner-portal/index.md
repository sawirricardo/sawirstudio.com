# Jetfly Owner Portal

Every aircraft owner’s costs, revenue and hours flown in one place

![my.jetfly.com, the owners' portal: costs per hour, revenue, hours flown, logbook and invoices](https://sawirstudio.com/assets/images/projects/jetfly-owner-portal.webp)

## The result

- **Owners see their aircraft in one place.** Costs, revenue, hours flown, the logbook and invoices, at my.jetfly.com.
- **Still in use.** The portal began as my.fly7.com and now opens as my.jetfly.com.
- **Two years of steady releases,** from November 2023 to December 2025.

## The situation

Jetfly flies Pilatus PC-12s, PC-24s and Falcon 7Xs across Europe, for aircraft owners and for charter. Owners want to know what their aircraft cost, what it earned and how much it flew, and those numbers sit in the operator’s flight and accounting systems.

## The task

Give every owner one place to see their aircraft’s numbers, kept in step with the operations systems, and let Jetfly’s team check each month’s logbook before owners see it.

## What we did

We were the main developer on the owners’ portal, working directly with Jetfly’s team.

- **Dashboards.** Costs per hour, revenue and hours flown, split between the owner, affiliated contracts and Fly7 (now Jetfly On-Demand), with a yearly summary.
- **The logbook.** Each flight with its costs, pilot rebilling and variable costs, checked by the team and then published to the owner.
- **Invoices.** Every invoice and expense file for the owner, searchable.
- **In step with operations.** Flights, contracts and crew names synchronised from Jetfly’s operations systems.
- **Admin.** Fuel surcharges, and roles and permissions for the team.

### Also for Jetfly

- **Reading on board.** Complimentary PressReader access for passengers, tied to their flight: a voucher per passenger, a link to share with co-passengers, trips with several legs, and access that ends after the flight.
- **Aircraft sales listings.** Fixes to listings and their images on mylittleplane.com.
- **A booking app prototype** for iPhone and Android.
