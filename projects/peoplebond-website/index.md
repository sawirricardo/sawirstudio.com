# PeopleBond Website

A bilingual website that sells the product as well as it works

![The peoplebond.id home page](https://sawirstudio.com/assets/images/projects/peoplebond-website.webp)

## The result

- **Rebuilt in under a week,** from November 23 to 26, 2026.
- **One site, two languages.** [peoplebond.id](https://peoplebond.id/) is in Indonesian and English.
- **A page for every question a buyer asks.** Each module, integration and industry has its own page, so an HR manager can check that PeopleBond handles their case before they ever talk to sales.
- **Free tools that bring people in.** Calculators for PPh 21 TER, net salary, THR, overtime and severance, plus a payslip and shift schedule generator, each following the current rules.
- **No lead lost.** A demo request is saved for the sales team before it opens a WhatsApp chat, so it isn’t lost when the chat never starts.

## The situation

PeopleBond’s product had outgrown its website. The site said little about what the software does, and it had no clear way to book a demo.

## The task

Explain every module and integration plainly, in Indonesian and English. Make booking a demo the obvious next step. And never promise something the product doesn’t do.

## What we did

- **Pages built from the product.** Before the payroll and tax pages went up, we checked what they say against the HCMS code: PPh 21 TER, the Coretax export, on-premise installs, the messaging integrations.
- **Industry pages.** Energy, engineering, manufacturing, food and hospitality, finance and retail, each with what PeopleBond handles for that kind of company.
- **A clear next step.** “Book a Demo” is on every page, and the form leads straight into a WhatsApp chat with the team.
- **Comfortable to read.** The site follows the visitor’s light or dark setting.
