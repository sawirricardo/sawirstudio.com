Sawirly

A privacy-first URL shortener with no tracking and links that can expire

Most URL shorteners track your clicks, show ads or collect personal data. Sawirly doesn't: it does not log or fingerprint your IP address, location or browser, and every link is clean and private by default.

Links can have a custom alias, run between a start and end date, or work for a single click only. Sawirly is free for personal use, and developers get a clean, privacy-respecting API.
