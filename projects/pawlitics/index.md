# Pawlitics

A cozy cartoon politics sim where a town of animals reacts to every policy

Bramblewood needs a governor. Will it be you?

Pawlitics is a cozy, cartoon politics sim. Pick a governor, set the policies, and watch a town full of animals react: bears in hard hats, chickens in bow ties, mice who live in shoeboxes and say it's nice, actually.
