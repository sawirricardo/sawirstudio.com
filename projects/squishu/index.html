Squishu

Squish dumplings. Beat the boss before the steam runs out.

Squishu is a cute clicker game about squishing food. Tap dumplings, onigiri, sushi, mochi, boba and more until they pop, then face the boss of each world before its timer runs out. Hire a squad of dumpling friends to keep squishing while you're away, and come back to a steamer full of coins.

  • Client Our own app
  • Platforms iPhone, iPad
