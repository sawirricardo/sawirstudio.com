# PeopleBond HCMS Application

HR and payroll that keeps up with Indonesian tax law, since 2019

![PeopleBond Dashboard screenshot](https://sawirstudio.com/assets/images/projects/peoplebond-hcms-application.webp)

## The result

- **Companies run their payroll on it.** PeopleBond’s customers include [Rekayasa Engineering, Eatwell Culinary (Ichiban Sushi, Ta Wan, Dapur Solo), Mercure Hotels, PNM and Bhinneka](https://peoplebond.id/clients/).
- **Payroll kept up with the law.** When PP 58/2023 brought the TER rates and tax filing moved to Coretax, PeopleBond’s payroll followed.

> Sawir Studio has built our HR platform and employee app with us since 2019. They don’t disappear after a release: when we need something changed, they already know the system and get it done.

PeopleBond

## The situation

PeopleBond sells HR and payroll software to Indonesian companies, in the cloud or on their own servers. Payroll is where software like this earns its keep or loses a customer: it has to be right every month, and Indonesia’s tax rules keep moving.

## The task

Keep payroll correct every time the rules change, across every module a company relies on, without breaking what customers already run.

## What we did

The web application covers employees, recruitment, timesheets, payroll, leave, competency, evaluation and training, with a documentation site for the companies that use it.

- **Tax as the law says.** PPh 21 with the TER rates from PP 58/2023, A, B or C by PTKP status, and the year recalculated at progressive rates in December.
- **Filing without retyping.** Tax forms come out ready for Coretax and e-Bupot, and BPJS reports are built in.
- **Approvals where people already are.** Leave requests can reach approvers on WhatsApp, Telegram or LINE.

### The call we keep making: carry it forward, don’t rewrite

Seven years in, a rewrite is always tempting. We keep choosing to upgrade what’s there instead: platform upgrades and new tax rules get handled as they come. A rewrite would freeze payroll changes for months while the tax rules keep moving.
