My Spiritual Journey

A church’s daily devotions, as easy to keep up as a workout

The result

  • 1,000+ downloads on Google Play, and on the App Store since June 2023.
  • Still growing, four years in. Built and looked after since 2022, with new releases still going out in 2026.

The situation

GBI WTC is a church in the Gereja Bethel Indonesia family. Sunday is one day a week; a healthy spiritual life is built on the other six.

The task

Give the congregation a daily companion for their spiritual life, something that feels as natural to open as a fitness app, and give the church one platform to run it from.

What we did

We built My Spiritual Journey for iPhone and Android, and the platform behind it.

  • Spiritual Workouts. Daily devotions and activities for specific parts of spiritual life, such as calling, character and family, with daily, weekly and monthly goals.
  • Badges and friends. A badge for each workout completed, and friends’ progress alongside your own, so people keep each other going.
  • Connected to the church. The church’s transformation classes and care cells, with their members and activities.
  • The Bible inside. Scripture in several translations, right where the devotion asks for it.
  • Safe sign-up. New accounts and password changes are confirmed with a code texted to the phone.

The same platform runs the rest of the church’s content: sermons, pastor’s messages, devotions for adults and kids, e-books, songs and church forms. And because a church app holds members’ personal details, it gets the same upkeep as any business system: security updates as they come out, and limits on how often a sign-up code can be requested.
